Hedge trimming that's done properly

Every Frankston hedge trimming job is tailored to the plant and the look you want. Our service covers:

  • Precise shaping for straight tops, flat faces and clean, defined lines
  • Height and width maintenance to keep hedges within a manageable shape
  • Ongoing scheduled trims that keep growth dense and tidy
  • Careful work around fences, windows and garden features
  • A full clean-up of the work area before we leave

The hedges we trim — and the ones we don't

We trim standard domestic hedges such as box, lilly pilly and murraya borders, along with screening hedges that can be worked safely from ground level or a short step.

We do not trim tall or oversized hedges. Very high screens, large conifers and anything needing elevated access are outside what we take on — if your hedge falls into that category we'll tell you straight away when you request a quote, rather than wasting your time.

When is the best time to trim hedges in Frankston?

Most hedges in the Frankston area respond best to a trim in late spring and again in late summer, after the main flush of new growth. A light trim in between keeps everything sharp. Trimming at the right time encourages dense, healthy regrowth rather than stressing the plant.

Regular scheduled hedge trimming in Frankston

Hedges look their best when they're trimmed on a schedule rather than rescued once a year. We set a regular trimming interval to suit the plant, and many customers have it looked after alongside their recurring lawn mowing visit or as part of ongoing grounds maintenance.
