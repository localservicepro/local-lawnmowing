Grounds maintenance vs lawn mowing — what's the difference?

Lawn mowing covers cutting, edging and line-trimming. Depending on the property and mowing conditions, grass clippings are either mulched back into the lawn or placed in your green waste bin. Grounds maintenance is broader ongoing care built around that regular mow — plus hedge and shrub trimming, pruning and weed spraying. If you only need the lawn done, see our lawn mowing service.

A schedule built around your regular mow

Grounds maintenance runs on the same rhythm as our mowing: fortnightly from September to June, moving to every three to four weeks over winter as growth slows. The extra jobs are slotted into those visits as the property needs them. We turn up when we say we will, do the job properly, and leave everything clean — backed by 50+ verified 5-star reviews.
