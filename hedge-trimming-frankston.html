Hedge trimming that's done properly

Every Frankston hedge trimming job is tailored to the plant and the look you want. Our service covers:

  • Precise shaping for straight tops, flat faces and clean, defined lines
  • Height and width reduction to bring overgrown hedges back under control
  • Ongoing maintenance trims that keep growth dense and tidy
  • Careful work around fences, windows and garden features
  • Full clean-up and green-waste removal — nothing left behind

Any hedge, any size

From compact box and lilly pilly borders to towering photinia and conifer screens, we have the equipment and experience to trim hedges of any size safely. Overgrown or neglected hedges are no problem — we'll reduce them back to a neat, manageable shape and then keep them there with a regular schedule.

When is the best time to trim hedges in Frankston?

Most hedges in the Frankston area respond best to a trim in late spring and again in late summer, after the main flush of new growth. A light tidy-up in between keeps everything sharp. Trimming at the right time encourages dense, healthy regrowth rather than stressing the plant.
