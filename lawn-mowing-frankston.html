What's included in every mow

A Frankston lawn mowing visit from us is never just a quick cut. Every scheduled service includes:

  • Precision mowing at the correct height for your grass type and the season
  • Crisp edging along paths, driveways, garden beds and the nature strip
  • Line-trimming around trees, fences and hard-to-reach corners
  • Full green-waste removal — we take the clippings away and leave the property tidy
  • A quick check for any issues, so small problems don't become big ones

Weekly, fortnightly or one-off mowing

Fortnightly lawn mowing is our most popular schedule in Frankston, and it keeps most lawns looking their best year round. Prefer a one-off tidy-up before a party, an inspection or an open-for-inspection? We handle those too, along with holiday lawn mowing while you're away.

Local knowledge that keeps your lawn healthy

Frankston's temperate coastal climate suits warm-season grasses like Buffalo and Kikuyu, which surge after summer rain and slow right down in winter. We adjust cutting heights through the year — never scalping your lawn in a heatwave — so it stays green, thick and resistant to weeds.

Lawn mowing FAQs

How often should I mow my lawn in Frankston?+

Mow weekly to fortnightly through the spring and summer growing season (September to March), then every three to four weeks over the cooler months. Warm-season grasses like Buffalo and Kikuyu grow fastest after summer rain.

Do you remove the grass clippings?+

Yes. Every lawn mowing visit includes edging and full green-waste removal — we leave your lawn cut, tidy and clear of clippings.

Do you offer one-off and holiday lawn mowing?+

Yes. As well as regular weekly and fortnightly schedules, we handle one-off tidy-ups, pre-sale mows and holiday lawn mowing while you're away in Frankston.
