Frequently asked questions

Lawn Mowing in Frankston — Your Questions Answered

How much does lawn mowing cost in Frankston?+

Most Frankston lawn mowing jobs range from about $45 to $90 per visit, depending on lawn size, access and how often you book. Regular fortnightly customers pay less per visit than one-off cuts. We always give a firm, no-obligation quote before we start — no surprises.

How often should I mow my lawn in Frankston's climate?+

In Frankston's temperate climate, mow weekly to fortnightly through the spring and summer growing season (September to March), then every three to four weeks over the cooler months. Warm-season grasses like Buffalo and Kikuyu grow fastest after summer rain, so a regular schedule keeps them healthy and tidy.

Do you offer fortnightly lawn mowing in Frankston?+

Yes — fortnightly lawn mowing is our most popular schedule in Frankston. We set a recurring visit that suits your lawn, turn up reliably, and handle everything from edging to green-waste removal so you never have to think about it.

Are you insured and do you service commercial properties?+

Yes. Local Lawn Mowing & Maintenance is a registered and fully insured business, established in 2014. We service residential homes as well as commercial and strata properties across Frankston and the Mornington Peninsula.

What's included in grounds maintenance vs lawn mowing?+

Lawn mowing covers cutting, edging and green-waste removal. Grounds maintenance is broader ongoing care — mowing plus hedge trimming, weeding, pruning, garden-bed tidy-ups and rubbish removal — to keep the whole property looking sharp all year round.
