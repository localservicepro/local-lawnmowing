What's included in grounds maintenance vs lawn mowing?+

Lawn mowing covers cutting, edging and line-trimming. Depending on the property and the mowing conditions, grass clippings are either mulched back into the lawn or placed in your green waste bin. Grounds maintenance is broader ongoing care built around that regular mow — hedge and shrub trimming, pruning and weed spraying — to keep the whole property tidy year round.
